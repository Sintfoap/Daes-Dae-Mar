# Daes Dae Mar

*"The Great Game" — a strategy game where every faction is playing it.*

**Daes Dae Mar** is a strategy game set in the world of Robert Jordan's *The
Wheel of Time*. It crosses two design lineages:

- **[Conquest of Elysium 5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conquest_of_Elysium)** —
  a sprawling, simply-illustrated fantasy strategy game where dozens of
  wildly asymmetric factions compete on a shared map, each AI faction acting
  with real agency rather than as scripted opposition.
- **[Total War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_War_(video_game_series))** —
  real battles fought on real ground, where the strategic layer sets the
  stage and the player wins or loses battles through tactical decisions, not
  just army-size math.

The hybrid: a **CoE5-style strategic layer** (simple iconography, many
asymmetric AI factions, resource and territory management) feeding into a
**grid-based tactical battle layer** with terrain to fight around, armies
entering the field from whichever edge matches the direction they actually
attacked from on the strategic map, real (if bounded and asymmetric)
freedom in how both sides deploy into that terrain, and live order-giving
so the player can micro a battle as it happens instead of just watching
two blobs collide.

The Wheel of Time setting supplies the factions, the power scaling (the One
Power, and everything that comes with wielding it), the units, and the
resources.

## Project status

**Phase 1 — Concept & Design Bible.** No code yet, by design. We're
deliberately spending Phase 1 entirely on paper: nailing the design so that
when Phase 2 (technical prototyping) starts, we're validating a real concept
instead of discovering design problems in code. See `ROADMAP.md` for the
full phase breakdown.
